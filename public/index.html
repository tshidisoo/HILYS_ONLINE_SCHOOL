How lessons work

Short, live and at home

30 minutes on Zoom
Long enough to learn, short enough to keep a young child focused. All your child needs is a laptop or tablet with a camera.
One-to-one or a small group
Choose private lessons, or a group of up to 3 children of a similar age and level. Brothers, sisters, cousins and friends make great groups.
Pictures, book pages and videos
Everything is shared on screen, so your child can see and hear each new word as they learn it.
A parent nearby
You're welcome to stay close during the lesson. It helps your child focus, and you see their progress for yourself.
Times that suit UK families
Lessons run after school on weekdays and at weekends. Booking times are shown in your own time zone.

Questions parents ask

Good to know before you start

My child only speaks English. Is it too late?

No. Children from age 6 pick up a new language quickly, especially when they hear it at home too. Beginner lessons start from greetings and family words.

Is this Zimbabwean or South African isiNdebele?

Zimbabwean isiNdebele, the language spoken in Bulawayo and Matabeleland, taught by a Zimbabwean teacher.

What does my child need for lessons?

A laptop or tablet with a camera, a microphone, a steady internet connection and the free Zoom app. Headphones help in a busy home.

Can brothers and sisters learn together?

Yes. Siblings and cousins of a similar age and level make an ideal group of up to 3, at the group price of $8 per child per lesson.

What happens if we miss a lesson?

Tell us at least 24 hours before and we'll move it to another time. Our parent policies explain missed lessons, power cuts and payments.

How will I know my child is making progress?

You can sit in on any lesson, and you'll hear new words and songs at home. The recipes and stories are made to share with the whole family.
