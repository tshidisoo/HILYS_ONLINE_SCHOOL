How lessons, payments and safety work

The short version of what you can expect from HILYS, and what we ask of you. If anything here is unclear, message us before your first lesson.

  • Safety
  • Missed lessons
  • Power & internet
  • Payments

Keeping your child safe online

  • An adult should be at home and nearby for every lesson. You're welcome to sit in.
  • Your child joins from a shared room, such as the living room or kitchen, not a bedroom.
  • We only message parents. We never contact your child directly by phone, WhatsApp, email or social media.
  • Every lesson uses a private Zoom link with a waiting room. Please don't share the link.
  • We don't record lessons. If you'd like a recording to help your child practise, ask and we'll only record with your written permission.
  • We never post photos, videos or your child's name online without your written permission.

If your child misses a lesson

  • Tell us at least 24 hours before and we'll move the lesson to another free slot in the same month, or the first week of the next.
  • With less than 24 hours' notice, or if your child doesn't join, the lesson counts as used.
  • If your child joins late, the lesson still ends at its usual time so the next child isn't kept waiting.
  • In a group lesson, the lesson goes ahead if one child is missing. We can't move a group lesson for one family.

Illness and emergencies happen. Message us as soon as you can and we'll do our best to help.

Power cuts and dropped connections

  • If we have to cancel a lesson, you get a replacement lesson at no cost.
  • If the connection drops on our side, we reconnect as fast as we can. If more than 10 minutes of the lesson is lost, we make up the lesson in full.
  • If the connection drops on your side, we wait for your child to rejoin, but the lesson still ends at its usual time.
  • If we can't reconnect, we'll message you on WhatsApp straight away to agree a new time.

Paying for lessons

  • Packages are paid monthly, in USD, before the first lesson of the month.
  • You can pay by Mukuru, EcoCash Diaspora or Western Union. We'll send you the payment details when you join.
  • After paying, send us your payment reference on WhatsApp so we can match it to your child.
  • Your child keeps their regular lesson times for as long as you keep paying each month.
  • Extra lessons cost the same as lessons in your package: $12 one-to-one, or $8 per child in a group.

Changing or stopping lessons

  • You can change your package or stop at the end of any month. Just tell us before you would next pay.
  • Lessons already paid for in the current month aren't refunded, but your child can still use them.
  • If we ever have to stop teaching, we'll refund any lessons you've paid for and haven't used.
