What your child learns

From the first vowel to reading a story

Sounds and letters

The five vowels and 20 consonant sounds, including the three clicks: c, q and x.

Reading and writing

Syllables, picture words and sentences, with letters to trace and missing letters to fill in.

Culture

A "Did you know?" in every lesson: respect for elders, mopane worms, the cattle kraal and more.

Inside a lesson

Every lesson follows the same shape

Children settle quickly when they know what comes next.

Hello and remember
The same greeting every time, then a quick look back at the last lesson.
New sound
A letter card, how to make the sound, and its syllables.
Words and reading
Picture words, lines from the book, then sentences or a short story.
Talk and a game
Talking about a picture, and a short game that uses the new sound.
Did you know?
A piece of Ndebele culture linked to the lesson.
Finish it, write, home task
Fill in the missing letters, trace the letter, and a small task to do at home.
