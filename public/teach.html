Teach with HILYS

Help children abroad keep their isiNdebele.

We teach children of Ndebele families living outside Zimbabwe, live on Zoom. If you speak isiNdebele well and enjoy teaching children, we'd like to hear from you.

How to apply See what we teach
  • Teach from home
  • One-to-one or groups of up to 3
  • 30-minute lessons
Every lesson starts Salibonani! · Linjani? Hello! · How are you all?
Who we're looking for

Good isiNdebele, and patience with children

A strong speaker

You speak, read and write Zimbabwean isiNdebele confidently, and can explain it in English.

Good with children

Our learners are aged 6 and up. Many are starting from scratch, so warmth and patience matter most.

Set up for Zoom

A laptop with a camera, a quiet place to teach, and a plan for power cuts and slow internet.

What HILYS gives you

You teach. We handle the rest.

Lessons ready to teach
Slides, flashcards and a step-by-step plan for every lesson, so you don't start from a blank page.
Families who want to learn
We find the families, book the lessons and match each child to the right level.
Payments handled
Parents pay HILYS. Your pay is agreed with you in writing before your first lesson.
Someone to ask
A senior teacher watches your first lessons and helps you settle into the HILYS way of teaching.
How to join

Four steps to your first class

  1. Step 1

    Say hello

    Message us with a little about yourself and your teaching.

  2. Step 2

    Teach a trial lesson

    You teach a short lesson from our materials while a senior teacher watches.

  3. Step 3

    Safety and training

    You read and agree to our child safety rules, and learn how HILYS lessons run.

  4. Step 4

    Start teaching

    We match you with your first children, at times that suit you.
