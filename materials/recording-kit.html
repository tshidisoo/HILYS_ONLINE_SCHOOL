Keys: → next slide, ← back, space starts or pauses the clock, R marks the slide as recorded, + and − change the text size.

    Slides

      Before you record
      • Open the lesson's slides on the screen you are recording, and this page on a second screen or a phone.
      • Have the book open at the lesson's pages.
      • Record one slide at a time. Stopping between slides makes mistakes easy to redo.
      • After each question, stay quiet for 2 seconds so the child can answer.
      • The clock shows the time planned for each slide. It is a guide, not a rule.
