# Research: competitors and the best software for this trade

Written 2026-11-15 for the HILYS ecosystem build. HILYS teaches isiNdebele online to children of Ndebele families abroad: live lessons on Zoom from Zimbabwe, and a Book 1 video course in preparation. Competitor names appear here only, never in the product.

Sources were web search summaries; prices are as reported by those sources and may have changed.

## 1. Direct competitors (isiNdebele and Southern African languages for diaspora children)

| Provider | What they sell | Price seen | Strengths | Gaps HILYS can fill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Individual tutors on marketplaces (italki, AmazingTalker, Teachme2) | One-to-one lessons; games, songs, story books for children | Marketplace tutors mostly £7–20 an hour | Easy to book; reviews | No curriculum or levels; quality varies; no progress record for parents |
| Udemy beginner and intermediate isiNdebele courses | Recorded course for diaspora families | One-off course price | Self-paced, cheap | No teacher, no feedback, no child-specific structure |
| Vambo Academy (South Africa) | Live lessons and a subscription across many African languages | 2022: R180 per 45-minute lesson; subscription model | Breadth; pay-per-lesson and subscription | Adults first; isiNdebele not clearly current; not child-focused |
| Speakndebele.com | Project to make isiNdebele accessible to diaspora children and teenagers | Not found | Same audience | No live teaching found |
| Shona equivalents (Chii? app, ZimbOriginal) | Self-directed app; storytelling and buddy pairing | Not found | Shows diaspora parents pay for heritage-language apps | Shona only |

Sources: [Udemy](https://www.udemy.com/course/ndebeleforbeginners/), [italki](https://www.italki.com/en/teacher/17712000), [Teachme2](https://www.teachme2.com/ndebele-language-online-lessons), [Vukuzenzele](https://www.vukuzenzele.gov.za/learn-african-languages-easily-online), [Disrupt Africa](https://disrupt-africa.com/2022/07/29/how-sas-vambo-academy-is-enabling-the-online-learning-of-african-languages/), [News24](https://www.news24.com/citypress/news/social-entrepreneur-scoops-international-award-nomination-20220613), [Nehanda Radio](https://nehandaradio.com/tag/chii/), [AmazingTalker](https://www.amazingtalker.co.uk/tutors/shona/academic).

**Finding:** nobody combines a structured children's curriculum, live teaching, recorded lessons and a visible progress record for parents. That combination is HILYS's position.

## 2. Best software built for tutoring schools

| Product | Best at | Ideas worth adopting |
|---|---|---|
| Teachworks | Multi-tutor centres | Internal and shared lesson notes (shared ones emailed to families); attendance tracking; tutor pay rates and payroll reports; rule-based billing; parent and student portal |
| TutorCruncher | Agencies with many freelance tutors | Client balance = payments minus completed lessons and extra charges; prepaid credit requests; ad hoc charges that can bill the client, pay the tutor, or both; lesson reports required before invoicing |
| TutorBird | Solo tutors | Low cost; SMS reminders; simple setup |
| My Music Staff | Small studios | Simple family accounts and lesson calendar |
| Outschool (marketplace) | Children's online classes | Parent app: schedule, join the lesson, message the teacher; parent-teacher messages used for progress check-ins |

Sources: [Capterra: Teachworks](https://www.capterra.co.uk/software/136856/teachworks), [Teachworks blog](https://blog.teachworks.com/2023/01/welcome-to-teachworks-take-our-quick-tour/), [TutorCruncher: client balances](https://help.tutorcruncher.com/en/articles/8229539-client-balances), [TutorCruncher: ad hoc charges](https://help.tutorcruncher.com/en/articles/8228146-ad-hoc-charges), [TutorCruncher: credit requests](https://help.tutorcruncher.com/en/articles/14183175-creating-credit-requests), [ClassCard 2026 roundup](https://www.classcardapp.com/blog/best-tutoring-management-software-in-2026), [Tutorbase comparison](https://tutorbase.com/compare/mymusicstaff-vs-teachworks), [Outschool app support](https://support.outschool.com/en/articles/11944008-outschool-mobile-app-support), [Outschool communication tools](https://support.outschool.com/en/articles/2535171-outschool-s-communication-tools).

## What we adopt, and why

1. **Shared and private lesson notes** (Teachworks). Teachers write a private note and a parent note after each lesson; parents see only the parent note. Parents abroad cannot sit in every lesson.
2. **Attendance statuses that follow the parent policy** (HILYS policy, Teachworks). Taught, Moved with 24 hours' notice, Missed (counts as used), Cancelled by HILYS, Cut short by power or internet. Make-ups owed are tracked, as the tracker already does.
3. **Lesson report before pay** (TutorCruncher). A lesson counts for teacher pay only once the teacher has recorded attendance and notes.
4. **Teacher pay from taught lessons** (Teachworks, TutorCruncher). A payout statement per teacher per month from their lessons and rate.
5. **Family account with derived balance** (TutorCruncher). What a family owes is calculated from invoices and recorded payments, never typed.
6. **Monthly package invoices** (HILYS prices). Invoices come from the price book's packages; extra lessons at the per-lesson price.
7. **Parent app: schedule in the family's time zone, lesson notes, invoices, messages** (Outschool). Families are in other countries; times must show in theirs.
8. **Curriculum progress and certificates** (HILYS's own Book 1 material). The thing no competitor shows: which Book 1 lessons a child has done, and the end-of-book check before the certificate.
9. **Diaspora payment methods.** Record Mukuru, EcoCash Diaspora and Western Union payments with their reference, because that is how HILYS families pay today.
