# Beginner 4: Colours and food

**Goal:** the child can name 5 colours and 3 Ndebele foods.
**Culture:** recipe, making isitshwala with a parent (the parent does the cooking).
**Flashcards:** set *Lesson 4*.

> Draft isiNdebele: check colour words and food names before teaching.

## Review (from Lesson 3)
Count to 10 together. Ask *Ulemnyaka emingaki?* (How old are you?)

## New words

| isiNdebele | English |
|---|---|
| -bomvu | red |
| -luhlaza | green / blue |
| -phuzi | yellow |
| -mhlophe | white |
| -mnyama | black |
| isitshwala | thick maize porridge, eaten with relish |
| inyama | meat |
| imbuya | leafy greens |
| amasi | sour milk |

**Sentence pattern:** *Isitshwala simhlophe.* (Isitshwala is white.)

## Send to the parent 2 days before
> In our next lesson [child] will help make isitshwala with you! Please have mealie meal, water, a pot and a wooden spoon ready near the laptop. **You do the stove and hot water**, and [child] can measure, pour and watch. If cooking during the lesson doesn't work for you, cook it together at the weekend and we'll talk about it in the lesson instead.

## Lesson flow

1. **Greeting and review (6 min).** Count to 10 and say your age.
2. **Colours (8 min).** Show colour cards. Play **"Find it!"**: say a colour and the child runs to find something that colour at home, then shows it on camera.
3. **Recipe: isitshwala (12 min).** Move the laptop to the kitchen if the parent agrees.
   - Name each thing in isiNdebele as it appears: *amanzi* (water), *impuphu* (mealie meal), *ibhodo* (pot).
   - The child counts spoonfuls in isiNdebele.
   - Talk about who makes isitshwala in your family, and what it's eaten with.
   - If they aren't cooking during the lesson, show pictures and ask what colour everything is.
4. **Home task (2 min).** At dinner, tell a parent the colour of each food on your plate in isiNdebele.
5. **Goodbye (2 min).** Praise everything they've learned in 4 lessons. *Sala kuhle!*

## After Lesson 4
Send the parent a short progress note on WhatsApp. List what the child can now say, and name one thing to practise at home. This is also a good time to ask for feedback and a testimonial.
