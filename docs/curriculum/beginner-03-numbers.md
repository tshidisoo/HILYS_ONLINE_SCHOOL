# Beginner 3: Numbers 1–10

**Goal:** the child can count to 10 and say how old they are.
**Culture:** recipe, counting maputi (popcorn) together.
**Flashcards:** set *Lesson 3*.

> Draft isiNdebele: number words vary between sources and between Zimbabwean and South African usage. Please replace them with the forms you use.

## Review (from Lesson 2)
*umama, ubaba, ugogo, ubabamkhulu, Lo ngu…*

## New words

| Number | isiNdebele (counting) |
|---|---|
| 1 | okukodwa |
| 2 | okubili |
| 3 | okuthathu |
| 4 | okune |
| 5 | okuhlanu |
| 6 | isithupha |
| 7 | isikhombisa |
| 8 | isificaminwembili |
| 9 | isificamunwemunye |
| 10 | itshumi |

**Sentence pattern:** *Ngilemnyaka eyi-7.* (I am 7 years old.)

## Send to the parent the day before
> Tomorrow we're counting with maputi (popcorn)! If you can, have a small bowl of popcorn ready, already popped and cooled, plus 2 small plates. Ngiyabonga!

## Lesson flow

1. **Greeting and review (6 min).** Ask *Lo ngubani?* while showing family cards.
2. **New words (10 min).** Count on your fingers together, slowly, then fast, then in a whisper. Play **"How many?"**: hold up some fingers and the child says the number.
3. **Recipe: counting maputi (9 min).** The child counts maputi from the bowl onto two plates: *okukodwa, okubili…* up to 10. Then the parent asks for a number (e.g. "*okuhlanu* for me!") and the child gives them that many. The child can eat what's left on their plate.
   - Talk about maputi as a favourite snack in Zimbabwe.
4. **Home task (3 min).** Count stairs, spoons or toys at home in isiNdebele. The parent sends you a 10-second voice note of the child counting.
5. **Goodbye (2 min).** *Sala kuhle!*
