# Beginner month 2: lessons 9–16

Month 2 builds on month 1. Children now know greetings, family, numbers, colours, food, animals, body parts and the home. They start to **put words into short sentences** and to understand more isiNdebele without English.

Every lesson keeps the same pattern (greeting → review → new words → culture → home task → goodbye). Each lesson below lists only what's new.

> **Check before teaching:** all isiNdebele here is a draft. Please correct it so it matches Zimbabwean isiNdebele as you teach it.

**Use more isiNdebele this month:** give instructions (*Lalela!* Listen! *Khuluma!* Speak! *Phinda!* Again!) in isiNdebele only, with gestures.

## Lesson 9: Days and weather
**Flashcards:** *Lesson 9*

| isiNdebele | English |
|---|---|
| uMvulo | Monday |
| uLwesibili | Tuesday |
| uLwesithathu | Wednesday |
| uLwesine | Thursday |
| uLwesihlanu | Friday |
| uMgqibelo | Saturday |
| iSonto | Sunday |
| Kuyatshisa | It's hot |
| Kuyaqanda | It's cold |
| Liyana | It's raining |

**Sentence pattern:** *Lamuhla ngu…* (Today is…). *Lamuhla kuyaqanda.* (Today it's cold.)

**Activity:** start every lesson from now on with a 1-minute **weather report**: the child looks out of the window and says the day and the weather in isiNdebele.
**Culture:** compare the weather in the UK and Zimbabwe today. Show a weather app for Bulawayo. Children are always surprised how different it is.
**Home task:** say the day and the weather to a parent every morning this week.

## Lesson 10: Clothes
**Flashcards:** *Lesson 10*

| isiNdebele | English |
|---|---|
| ihembe | shirt |
| ibhulukwe | trousers |
| ingubo | dress |
| izicathulo | shoes |
| isigqoko | hat |
| amasokisi | socks |
| ijezi | jumper |

**Sentence pattern:** *Ngigqoke ihembe elibomvu.* (I'm wearing a red shirt.) This reuses colours from lesson 4.

**Activity:** **"Dress up race."** Say *Gqoka isigqoko!* and the child runs to put on a hat and comes back to the camera. Then ask: what does the weather tell you to wear today? (Link to lesson 9.)
**Culture:** show photos of Ndebele beadwork (*ubuhlalu*) and traditional dress. Talk about when people wear them.
**Home task:** choose tomorrow's clothes with a parent, naming each one in isiNdebele.

## Lesson 11: At the market
**Flashcards:** *Lesson 11*

| isiNdebele | English |
|---|---|
| imakethe | market |
| imali | money |
| ukuthenga | to buy |
| ukuthengisa | to sell |
| ibhanana | banana |
| utamatisi | tomato |
| umbila | maize (corn) |
| amakhowa | mushrooms |

**Sentence patterns:** *Ngicela…* (Please may I have…). *Yimalini?* (How much is it?). *Ngiyabonga!*

**Activity:** **market role-play.** The child sets up a "stall" with real fruit or toys at home and sells to you, then you swap. Use numbers 1–10 from lesson 3 for prices.
**Culture:** describe a market in Bulawayo or a roadside stall in Zimbabwe. What's sold there that children won't see in a UK supermarket?
**Home task:** at the supermarket, the child names 3 things in isiNdebele and "buys" them.

## Lesson 12: Story day
**Flashcards:** review *Lesson 5* (animals)

No new word list this lesson. It's all about the story.

**Activity:** tell a new folktale, longer than before (5–6 minutes), using animals and words the child knows. Then:
1. Show 4 pictures from the story in the wrong order. The child puts them in order.
2. The child tells the story back using the pictures. Help with isiNdebele words.
3. The child invents a new ending.

**Culture:** explain that grandmothers traditionally told *izinganekwane* in the evening, and that some stories were only told at night.
**Home task:** tell the story to a parent or grandparent at bedtime.

## Lesson 13: Feelings
**Flashcards:** *Lesson 13*

| isiNdebele | English |
|---|---|
| Ngiyathokoza | I'm happy |
| Ngidabukile | I'm sad |
| Ngithukuthele | I'm angry |
| Ngiyesaba | I'm scared |
| Ngidiniwe | I'm tired |
| Ngilambile | I'm hungry |

**Sentence pattern:** *Unjani lamuhla?* → *Ngiyathokoza!* This gives the child more answers than *Ngiyaphila* from lesson 1.

**Activity:** **"Face game."** You pull a face and the child names the feeling, then swap. Then ask how a character in the story from lesson 12 felt.
**Culture:** a song with feelings, or a story where the hare is scared and then brave.
**Home task:** tell a parent how you feel at dinner every day this week, in isiNdebele.

## Lesson 14: Action words
**Flashcards:** *Lesson 14*

| isiNdebele | English |
|---|---|
| ukugijima | to run |
| ukudlala | to play |
| ukucula | to sing |
| ukudansa | to dance |
| ukuhamba | to walk |
| ukubala | to read / count |
| ukubhala | to write |
| ukulala | to sleep |

**Sentence pattern:** *Ngiyathanda ukudlala.* (I like playing.) *Angithandi ukulala!* (I don't like sleeping!) Children love the "don't like" form.

**Activity:** **charades.** The child acts out a word and you guess, then swap. Then play *uSija uthi…* from lesson 6 with action words: *uSija uthi gijima!*
**Home task:** tell a parent 3 things you like doing and 1 you don't.

## Lesson 15: Songs and dance from home
**Flashcards:** review *Lesson 14*

**Activity:** this lesson is about Ndebele music. Choose 1–2 songs you know well: a children's game song, a clapping song, or a traditional song.
1. Play a short video or recording of Ndebele singing and dancing (check that it suits children first).
2. Teach a clapping rhythm, then the words one line at a time.
3. Talk about when people sing: weddings, celebrations, working together, church.
4. The child dances along. Use action words from lesson 14: *Dansa! Cula!*

**Home task:** teach the song to someone at home and practise for the family show.

## Lesson 16: Family show 2
Follow the same format as [lesson 8](beginner-08-family-show.md), with harder challenges:

| Minutes | Part |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | Greeting plus the **weather report** (day and weather) |
| 3–8 | Flashcard challenge with month 2 cards (*Month 2* set) |
| 8–13 | **Market role-play** with a parent as the customer |
| 13–20 | The child **tells the story** from lesson 12 with pictures |
| 20–25 | **The song and dance** from lesson 15. Everyone joins in. |
| 25–30 | Praise, a short chat with the parent, and goodbye |

**After:** send the progress note (template in lesson 8). Suggest moving up to Intermediate after month 3 if the child is answering in sentences.
