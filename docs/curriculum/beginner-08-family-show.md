# Beginner 8: Family show (end of the first month)

**Goal:** the child shows the family everything they've learned, and the parent sees the progress.
**Culture:** the child retells a folktale opening and sings the songs from earlier lessons.
**Flashcards:** all sets, shuffled.

## Before the lesson
Send the parent this message 3–4 days before:

> Next lesson is [child]'s first **family show**! It's a chance for [child] to show you what they've learned this month. Invite anyone you like to watch: grandparents in Zimbabwe can join on the same Zoom link. Ngiyabonga!

Grandparents watching from home is a big motivation for children, and for parents to keep paying.

## Lesson flow

| Minutes | Part |
|---|---|
| 0–3 | The child greets everyone: *Salibonani! Ibizo lami ngu…* |
| 3–8 | **Flashcard challenge.** Shuffle all the sets, and the child names as many as they can in 3 minutes. The family claps for each one. |
| 8–13 | **My family.** The child shows their drawing from lesson 2 and introduces each person: *Lo ngumama…* |
| 13–18 | **Counting and colours.** The child counts to 10 and names colours they can see on the family members. |
| 18–24 | **Story time.** You say *Kwasukasukela…*, the child answers *Cosi!*, and the child tells as much of the hare story as they can, in English with isiNdebele words. |
| 24–27 | **Song.** Everyone sings the greeting song or the body song together. |
| 27–30 | You praise specific things, and the child says goodbye to everyone. |

## After the lesson
1. Send a written progress note on WhatsApp. Use the template below.
2. Ask for feedback, and a testimonial if they're happy (see `docs/whatsapp-messages.md`, messages 2 and 3).
3. Mark next month's package in the tracker.

### Progress note template
> **[Child]'s first month at HILYS**
> Can now say: greetings and their name, 8 family words, numbers to 10, 5 colours, 8 animals, 8 body parts, and words for home.
> Favourite part: [the song / the hare story / …]
> Keep practising at home: [one thing, e.g. greet grandma in isiNdebele on the phone]
> Next month: [the next topics]
> Ngiyabonga! — Sija
