# HILYS course plans

These are draft lesson plans for children aged 6–10 who are starting from scratch. Every lesson is 30 minutes on Zoom and includes one culture piece: a folktale, a song or a recipe.

> **Check before teaching:** all isiNdebele words, phrases and spellings here are drafts written in English first. Please correct them so they match Zimbabwean isiNdebele as you teach it. This matters most for numbers, family words and the folktale openings.

## Every lesson follows the same pattern

Young children settle faster when every lesson runs in the same order.

| Minutes | Part | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | **Greeting** (*Salibonani!*) | The same opening every time |
| 3–6 | **Review** | Go back over 3–5 words from last lesson using flashcards |
| 6–16 | **New words** | 6–8 new words with pictures, then a game |
| 16–25 | **Culture** | A folktale, a song or a recipe that uses the new words |
| 25–28 | **Home task** | One small thing to do with a parent before next lesson |
| 28–30 | **Goodbye** (*Sala kuhle!*) | The same closing every time |

## The 4 lessons

| # | Topic | Culture piece | Home task |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Greetings and names](beginner-01-greetings.md) | Song: a greeting song | Greet every family member in isiNdebele |
| 2 | [My family](beginner-02-family.md) | Folktale: uNogwaja and the animals | Draw your family and label them |
| 3 | [Numbers 1–10](beginner-03-numbers.md) | Recipe: counting maputi (popcorn) | Count things at home in isiNdebele |
| 4 | [Colours and food](beginner-04-colours-food.md) | Recipe: isitshwala with a parent | Tell a parent the colours on your plate |
| 5 | [Animals](beginner-05-animals.md) | Folktale: uNogwaja and the lion | Name 3 animals at home |
| 6 | [My body](beginner-06-body.md) | Action song, and *uSija uthi…* | Play *uMama uthi…* at home |
| 7 | [My home and my day](beginner-07-home.md) | Home tour, and the Zimbabwean *umuzi* | Label 3 things at home |
| 8 | [Family show](beginner-08-family-show.md) | Retell a story and sing for the family | Parent gets a progress note |

At 2 lessons a week, lessons 1–8 make up one month. The family show at the end is when parents see progress and decide on the next month.

## Materials
- **Flashcards:** `materials/flashcards.html`. Open it in Chrome, pick the lesson, and share that window on Zoom with "Share sound" ticked.
- **Pictures for the folktale:** 3–4 simple pictures (a hare, a lion, a tree, a river). You could use children's book pages or drawings.

## Progress at the end of the 4 lessons
By lesson 4, a Beginner child should be able to:
- greet someone and say their own name
- name 6 family members
- count to 10
- name 5 colours and 3 Ndebele foods
- answer *Cosi!* when a story starts
