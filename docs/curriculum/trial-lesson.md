# Free trial lesson (30 minutes)

The trial has two jobs. The child should enjoy it enough to want to come back, and you need to find their level: **Beginner**, **Intermediate** or **Conversation**. The parent should leave knowing which package to choose.

> **Check before using:** every isiNdebele word and phrase in this file is a draft. Please correct the spelling and wording so they match how you teach Zimbabwean isiNdebele.

## Before the lesson
- [ ] Ask the parent for the child's name, age, and which language the family speaks at home.
- [ ] Open the flashcards (`materials/flashcards.html`) on the **Trial** set.
- [ ] Have a short folktale ready to tell (2–3 minutes).
- [ ] Test screen sharing with sound.

## Lesson outline

| Time | What happens | What you're listening for |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Greet the child and parent. Say *Salibonani!* and see if the child answers. | Do they reply in isiNdebele, in English, or not at all? |
| 3–10 min | **Level check, step 1: understanding.** Ask the questions below in isiNdebele only, and wait. | Do they understand without English? |
| 10–15 min | **Level check, step 2: speaking.** Show 6 picture cards (family, animals, food) and ask *Yini le?* ("What is this?"). | Single words, full sentences, or English? |
| 15–23 min | **Folktale.** Tell a short story about uNogwaja (the hare), starting with *Kwasukasukela…* and asking the child to answer *Cosi!* | Do they follow the story? Can they retell one part? |
| 23–27 min | **Song or game.** Teach one line of a greeting song, or play "Simon says" with body parts. | Do they enjoy it? Will they try new words? |
| 27–30 min | Child says goodbye (*Sala kuhle!*). Then talk to the parent alone for 2 minutes. | |

### Step 1 questions (understanding)
Start easy and stop when the child is stuck.

1. *Unjani?* — How are you?
2. *Ibizo lakho ngubani?* — What is your name?
3. *Ulemnyaka emingaki?* — How old are you?
4. *Uhlala lobani ekhaya?* — Who do you live with at home?
5. *Uthanda ukudlalani?* — What do you like to play?
6. *Utshela ngelanga lakho lamuhla.* — Tell me about your day.

## Choosing the level

| If the child… | Level |
|---|---|
| Understands little or nothing, and answers only in English | **Beginner** |
| Understands questions 1–4 but answers in single words or in English | **Intermediate** |
| Understands everything and answers in isiNdebele sentences | **Conversation** |

## What to say to the parent (2 minutes)
1. One thing the child did well, e.g. "She understood every question I asked."
2. The level you'd recommend, and why.
3. Which package fits. For most families that's 2 lessons a week.
4. Whether a group might work, for example with siblings or cousins of a similar level.
5. Say you'll send the price list and payment details on WhatsApp today.
