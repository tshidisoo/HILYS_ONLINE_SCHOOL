# Book 1: end-of-book check (about 10 minutes)

A short spoken check before the child gets the Book 1 certificate. Do it live on Zoom, one child at a time, in or just after Lesson 35 (the family show). It should feel like part of the show, not a test: praise every try, and never say "wrong".

Everything the child reads here comes from Lesson 35, so it is all in the book. Share the Lesson 35 slides, or hold the lines up on screen.

> **For Sija:** the pass mark and the "what to do next" column are suggestions. Change them to match how you teach.

## The six parts

Tick a part when the child does it with little or no help. One prompt is fine. If you have to say the answer, leave it unticked.

| # | Ask the child to… | What to show or say | ✓ | If not yet, go back to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Say the 5 vowels** | a e i o u, then in a mixed order | ☐ | Lessons 3–4 |
| 2 | **Read syllables** from each group. Pick 5 at random from each line | ma be ci lo nu · ga fe si do wu · za ke ti yo qu · va je xi ho pu | ☐ | The review lesson for that group: 10, 16, 23 or 26 |
| 3 | **Make the 3 clicks** and read one syllable with each | c (ci) · q (qu) · x (xi) | ☐ | Lessons 7, 22 and 28 |
| 4 | **Read 8 words**, and say what 4 of them mean | umama · ubaba · ugogo · iluba · isisu · idada · izulu · ixoxo | ☐ | The lesson for the sound they stumble on |
| 5 | **Read 3 sentences** aloud | Bona umama. · Umama ubona iwuwu. · Ubaba usesibayeni. | ☐ | Lessons 19, 25 and 30–34 (the reading lessons) |
| 6 | **Talk a little**: answer the greeting, then do one more | *Salibonani!* → *Yebo, salibonani!* · *Linjani?* → *Siyaphila!* Then: count to 5 (kunye, kubili, kuthathu, kune, kuhlanu) **or** say today's weather | ☐ | Any lesson's hello and goodbye; Lesson 25 for counting; Lesson 33 for weather |

**Parts ticked:** ____ of 6

## What the result means

| Parts ticked | What to do |
|---|---|
| **5 or 6** | Give the certificate. The child is ready for Book 1b. |
| **3 or 4** | Give the certificate, and tell the parent which one or two things to practise. Use the "go back to" lessons above. |
| **0 to 2** | Hold the certificate for now. Repeat the lessons listed, then try the check again in 2 to 4 weeks. Tell the child they are "nearly there". |

## Afterwards

- **Tell the parent** one thing the child did well, and one thing to practise.
- **Certificate:** open `certificate.html` on the website, type the child's name and print it or save it as a PDF to send.
- **Tracker:** write the date and the score in the **Notes** column of the **Families** sheet, for example "Book 1 check 2027-03-14: 5/6".
- **Children on the video course only:** offer the check as their monthly live class (HILYS Plus), or as a single paid live lesson.
