# Conversation lessons 2–4

These follow on from [Conversation 1: My news](conversation-01-news.md). Every lesson starts with 5 minutes of **My news** and a **word catch** (writing down English words the child uses and teaching the isiNdebele). The proverb of the week continues.

> **Check before teaching:** all isiNdebele here is a draft.

---

## Conversation 2: Kids' debate

**Goal:** the child gives an opinion with reasons, and disagrees politely.
**Topic:** *Kungcono ukuhlala edolobheni kumbe emaphandleni?* (Is it better to live in the city or the countryside?)

| Phrase | English |
|---|---|
| Ngicabanga ukuthi … | I think that … |
| Ngoba … | Because … |
| Kodwa … | But … |
| Angivumelani lawe | I don't agree with you |
| Ngiyavumelana lawe | I agree with you |

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–8 | My news and word catch |
| 8–12 | Teach the debate phrases. |
| 12–24 | **Debate.** The child argues for the city (their life in the UK). You argue for the countryside (*emaphandleni* in Zimbabwe: space, cattle, family, stars at night). Then swap sides. |
| 24–28 | Proverb of the week |
| 28–30 | **Home task.** Ask a parent which they prefer, and why. |

Other debate topics for later: school uniform or no uniform? Is a dog or a cat better? Should children cook dinner once a week?

## Conversation 3: Interview a grandparent

**Goal:** the child prepares questions and holds a real conversation with an older relative.
**Culture:** family history, and respect language for elders.

### Arrange it beforehand
Ask the parent whether a grandparent or older relative can join the Zoom for 10 minutes. If nobody can, the child interviews you as if you were a grandparent.

| Phrase | English |
|---|---|
| Gogo / Mkhulu | Grandma / Grandpa (as a form of address) |
| Ngicela ukubuza … | Please may I ask … |
| Wakhulela ngaphi? | Where did you grow up? |
| Wawudlala imidlalo bani? | What games did you play? |
| Wawusiya esikolo njani? | How did you get to school? |

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | My news |
| 5–12 | The child writes 5 questions with your help, and practises respectful language. |
| 12–24 | **Interview.** The grandparent joins and the child asks the questions. You only help when they're stuck. |
| 24–28 | After the grandparent leaves, the child tells you the most interesting thing they learned. |
| 28–30 | **Home task.** Write or record 3 sentences about the grandparent's childhood. |

This is often the lesson parents remember most, and a natural moment to ask for a testimonial.

## Conversation 4: Make a news report

**Goal:** the child plans, records and shares a 1–2 minute isiNdebele report.
**Output:** a voice or video recording the family can send to relatives.

| Minutes | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | My news |
| 5–10 | Listen to a short isiNdebele radio or TV news clip that suits children, and talk about how newsreaders speak. |
| 10–18 | **Plan.** The child picks a topic (their school, their football team, the weather, a family event) and writes 5–6 sentences with your help. |
| 18–25 | **Rehearse.** The child practises out loud and you coach their pronunciation and confidence. |
| 25–28 | **Record.** The parent records the report on a phone. HILYS doesn't record it. |
| 28–30 | Goodbye. **Home task:** send the recording to grandparents. |

### Safety note
The family records and shares the report on their own phone. Don't ask for a copy, and don't post it anywhere, unless the parent gives written permission (see the parent policies).
