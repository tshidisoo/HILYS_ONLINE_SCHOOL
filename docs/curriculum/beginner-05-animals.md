# Beginner 5: Animals

**Goal:** the child can name 8 animals and say which one they like.
**Culture:** folktale, *uNogwaja and the lion* (or another uNogwaja trick story you know).
**Flashcards:** set *Lesson 5*.

> Draft isiNdebele: check spellings before teaching.

## Review (from Lesson 4)
Colours: play "Find it!" with 3 colours.

## New words

| isiNdebele | English |
|---|---|
| inja | dog |
| ikati | cat |
| inkomo | cow |
| imbuzi | goat |
| inkukhu | chicken |
| isilwane / ibhubesi | lion (*isilwane* can also mean any wild animal, so choose the word you use) |
| indlovu | elephant |
| unogwaja | hare |

**Sentence pattern:** *Ngiyayithanda inja.* (I like the dog.)

## Lesson flow

1. **Greeting and review (6 min).** "Find it!" with colours.
2. **New words (10 min).** Show the flashcards and make each animal's sound. Then play **"Who am I?"**: you make a sound or act out an animal, and the child names it in isiNdebele.
3. **Folktale (9 min).** *Kwasukasukela… Cosi!* Tell how the small, clever hare tricks the big, strong lion. Stop twice to ask *Kuzakwenzakalani?* (What will happen next?), and let the child guess in English or isiNdebele.
4. **Home task (3 min).** Find or draw 3 animals at home (a toy, a book, a pet) and name them for a parent.
5. **Goodbye (2 min).** *Sala kuhle!*

## Talking point
Ask the parent whether the family has a farm, or a grandparent with cattle or goats in Zimbabwe. Children connect strongly to stories about their own relatives.
