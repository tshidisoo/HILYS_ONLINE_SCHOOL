# Beginner 1: Greetings and names

**Goal:** the child can greet someone, ask how they are, and say their own name.
**Culture:** a greeting song.
**Flashcards:** set *Lesson 1*.

> Draft isiNdebele: check spellings before teaching.

## New words and phrases

| isiNdebele | English | Teaching tip |
|---|---|---|
| Salibonani | Hello (to someone you respect, or to more than one person) | Wave with your whole hand |
| Sawubona | Hello (to one person) | Point to one person |
| Unjani? | How are you? | Shrug your shoulders with a question face |
| Ngiyaphila | I am well | Thumbs up |
| Lawe unjani? | And you, how are you? | Point back at the other person |
| Ngiyabonga | Thank you | Hand on your chest |
| Ibizo lami ngu… | My name is… | Hand on your chest, then say your name |
| Sala kuhle | Goodbye (stay well) | Wave goodbye |

## Lesson flow

1. **Greeting (3 min).** Say *Salibonani!* with a big wave. Show the parent how to answer too.
2. **Introduce yourself (3 min).** Say *Ibizo lami nguSija.* Then point to the child and wait. Help them say *Ibizo lami ngu[name].*
3. **New words (10 min).** Go through the flashcards. Say each word 3 times and have the child copy you. Then play **"Which one?"**: say a word and the child does the action.
4. **Song (9 min).** Teach a simple greeting song you know, or a call and response:
   - You: *Salibonani!* Child: *Yebo, salibonani!*
   - You: *Linjani?* Child: *Siyaphila!*
   Repeat it faster each time and clap the rhythm together.
5. **Home task (3 min).** Before next lesson, greet every person at home in isiNdebele at least once. Ask the parent to answer in isiNdebele.
6. **Goodbye (2 min).** *Sala kuhle!* The child answers *Hamba kuhle!*

## If the child is shy
Greet a teddy or a family pet first. Shy children often speak to a toy before they speak to a new adult.
